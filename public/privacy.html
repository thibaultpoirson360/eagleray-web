Privacy Policy

How Eagle Ray Expeditions collects, uses, and protects the information you share with us.

This page is a working draft. Final wording is pending review by Eagle Ray Expeditions before the site goes live.

1. Information We Collect

When you reach out to plan an expedition, we collect what you choose to share with us : typically your name, contact details (WhatsApp number or email), preferred dates, group size, and trip preferences (activities, dietary needs, special occasions). This is normally shared directly through WhatsApp, email, or the trip-planning form on this site.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information only to plan, coordinate, and run your expedition : confirming availability, matching you with a boat and crew, arranging permits, and staying in touch before, during, and after your trip. We do not use your information for advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. How We Share Your Information

We share the minimum necessary details with the people who make your trip happen: your skipper and crew, and where required, marina or national park permit authorities. We do not share your information with marketers or data brokers.

4. Photography

Photos or videos taken during your trip are for your own memories by default. They're only used publicly (on this site or social media) with your explicit permission, requested separately by our storytelling crew.

5. Cookies & Tracking

When you first visit, a banner asks which optional cookies and services you allow. Nothing optional is loaded until you say yes. You can accept all, reject all, or choose by purpose, and change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page. We keep your choice for 12 months, then ask again.

Always on (strictly necessary). The site stores your cookie choice in your browser, and keeps a draft of the trip-planning form while you fill it in, so you don't lose your answers. Neither is sent to anyone else. These don't need your consent.

Analytics (only if you allow it). We use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google, to count visits and see which pages are read, so we can improve the site. Google sets cookies (such as _ga) and receives information about your visit, including your IP address, device and browser, and the pages you view. Data may be processed in the United States. See Google's privacy policy.

Marketing (only if you allow it). We use the Meta Pixel, provided by Meta Platforms (Facebook, Instagram), to measure whether our ads bring visitors to the site. Meta sets cookies (such as _fbp) and receives information about your visit, which it may combine with your Facebook or Instagram account under its own terms. Data may be processed in the United States. See Meta's privacy policy.

Embedded content (only if you allow it). Some pages show trip proposals from Mogu and forms from Tally inside the page. These services may set their own cookies and receive information about your visit. Until you allow embedded content, a placeholder is shown instead, with a link to open the content on their site.

If you turn a category off after allowing it, we delete the analytics and marketing cookies set on our site and stop loading those services. You can also clear cookies in your browser settings at any time.

6. Data Retention

We keep booking-related information for as long as needed to plan your trip and to meet basic accounting/legal record-keeping obligations afterward, then remove it upon request or after a reasonable period of inactivity.

7. Your Rights

You can ask us at any time to see what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. To make a request, contact us using the details below : we'll respond as quickly as we can.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to hello@eagleray.expedition or via WhatsApp at +52 55 2741 9922.
