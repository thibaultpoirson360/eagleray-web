Privacy Policy

How Eagle Ray Expeditions collects, uses, and protects the information you share with us.

This page is a working draft. Final wording is pending review by Eagle Ray Expeditions before the site goes live.

1. Information We Collect

When you reach out to plan an expedition, we collect what you choose to share with us : typically your name, contact details (WhatsApp number or email), preferred dates, group size, and trip preferences (activities, dietary needs, special occasions). This is normally shared directly through WhatsApp, email, or the trip-planning form on this site.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information only to plan, coordinate, and run your expedition : confirming availability, matching you with a boat and crew, arranging permits, and staying in touch before, during, and after your trip. We do not use your information for advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. How We Share Your Information

We share the minimum necessary details with the people who make your trip happen: your skipper and crew, and where required, marina or national park permit authorities. We do not share your information with marketers or data brokers.

4. Photography

Photos or videos taken during your trip are for your own memories by default. They're only used publicly (on this site or social media) with your explicit permission, requested separately by our storytelling crew.

5. Cookies & Tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or third-party pixels. The only piece of information the site stores in your browser is your selected language (English, Spanish, or French), saved locally so the site remembers your preference on your next visit : it is never sent to us or anyone else.

6. Data Retention

We keep booking-related information for as long as needed to plan your trip and to meet basic accounting/legal record-keeping obligations afterward, then remove it upon request or after a reasonable period of inactivity.

7. Your Rights

You can ask us at any time to see what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. To make a request, contact us using the details below : we'll respond as quickly as we can.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to hello@eagleray.expedition or via WhatsApp at +52 55 6809 0942.
